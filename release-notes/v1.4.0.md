## What's new

### Fixture Studio

The big one: make or fix any fixture, with a live 3D twin of it. No real light needed.

- **Start from anything**: type the DMX chart into a table (pasting a chart from a PDF fills it in), drop the manual or a fixture file (GDTF, Open Fixture Library, QLC+), search the web for the manual, or start from a kind of light and pick its channel count from the usual layouts.
- **Kinds of light for everything**: PARs, moving heads, bars, tubes, panels, lasers and effects, plus string/festoon lights, fog and haze machines, special effect machines (sparks, flames, bubbles), dimmer packs and "Something else" for anything that doesn't fit. Picking a kind opens its layouts right beside the list, so you can hop between kinds and compare.
- **Your own kinds of light**: save any fixture you build as a kind (an LED flex stand, a long string of lights, something none of the built-in kinds cover) and it's listed with the others as the starting point for the next one.
- **Claude** (with your API key connected) makes a fixture from its name or a description, reads manuals, and changes the open fixture when you describe the change.
- **See it working**: a 3D twin in a test room and a schematic of every channel, both lit live as you move sliders. Ready-made tests for PARs, washes, moving heads, bars, tubes, retro/multi-cell lights, lasers, derbies and GigBars, with one-click fixes.
- **LED rings**, more channel types, DMX modes, batch edits and a Problems list.
- **Fix fixtures from SoundSwitch** that show up wrong (e.g. sticks and battens showing as PARs): Save & add to show makes your version stand in; Back to the original undoes it.
- **Share**: export a `.svfixture` (opens in the app, fully editable) or an Open Fixture Library file.

### Also in this release

- **Lights on speakers**: put a light on top of a column PA, a speaker on a pole, a sub or a sub stack, or on a bracket on the speaker pole.
- **Lights inside a totem**: one on the base plate pointing up, one under the top plate pointing down. A white scrim glows with the colour, a black scrim keeps it hidden, and bare truss catches the light on the metal. The beams stop at the plates, like the real thing.
- **String / festoon lights** that hang from a truss with a gentle sag, each bulb lit on its own.
- **Betopper and Rockville** fixtures in the Library (PARs, washes, moving heads, bars, spiders, effects and fog machines), written from their user manuals.
- **About 1,600 more fixtures** in the Library from QLC+'s fixture library.
- Fixed: the Room colour menu closing while picking a colour.
