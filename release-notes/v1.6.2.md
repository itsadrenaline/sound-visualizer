## What's new

### Dragging onto the ceiling

- **Up onto the ceiling:** drag a light up, past where the floor ends, and it goes onto the ceiling. A ring shows where it will hang, like the rings on a truss. Let go and it hangs there, upside down.
- **Along the ceiling:** drag a light that's on the ceiling and it moves along the ceiling, staying inside the room. Move to Back, Centre, Front, Left and Right keep it up there too.
- **Back down:** drag it down below the horizon and it comes down to stand on the floor.
- **From the Library:** drop a light from the Library onto the ceiling and it hangs there.
- **Right-click** still has **Hanging from the ceiling** and **Upright**.
- **Fixed:** lights and disco balls on the ceiling sometimes hung a little below it, because the room grew taller around them. They now sit right at the ceiling.

### ADJ Starburst

- **UV now turns on.** SoundSwitch's Starburst names its UV (or purple), yellow and ball-motor channels in a way the app didn't recognise. These are now recognised by name, for any SoundSwitch fixture. UV is also drawn a stronger violet.
- **The ball turns** with its rotation channel, using the speeds from ADJ's manual: clockwise, stop, or anticlockwise.
- **Its beams land as moving spots.** Each beam lands as a spot on the floor, walls and ceiling, and the spots sweep round the room as the ball turns, like a disco ball.

### More objects

- **Photo booth with backdrop:** a selfie or photo booth with a backdrop on a frame (white, black or sequins), a camera kiosk in front and two soft lights on stands.
- **LED dance floor patterns:** choose Mix, Rainbow chase, Checkers, Ripple, **Sparkle** (LEDs flashing in colours), **Starfield** (white twinkles on black) or Colour fade. There's a ready-made "Sparkle dance floor" too.
- **Room lights**, a new section in Objects. These are lights that belong to the room, not the show:
  - **Chandelier**, 24 to 60 in, with crystals and candle bulbs.
  - **Pendant lights:** bare bulbs on cords of different lengths.
  - **Ceiling drape with fairy lights:** fabric swagged across the ceiling, the lights twinkling.
  - **Wall light:** a sconce washing light up and down the wall.

  Chandeliers and pendants hang from the ceiling, or from a truss bar you put them under.

  **They follow the room light.** Turn the room's light up (house lights) and they come up. Take it down and they dim. Each has **Light on** to switch it off, and **Dims with the room light** to keep it at full. They also light the room around them: a pool under a chandelier, a wash up and down the wall from a sconce. The photo booth's soft lights stay at full unless you change that.

**Pipe & drape** was already there, under Objects ▸ Room & decor.
