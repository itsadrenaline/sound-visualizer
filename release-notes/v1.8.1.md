## What's new

### LED rings on moving heads show their colour

Many moving heads have small LED rings that add colour but don't throw a beam of their own. They now glow on the fixture in the colour and brightness you set, so you can check your colours while programming.

- **Betopper LM1915P:** each of the 19 lenses has a ring of small RGB LEDs (the manual calls them "aux"). They are now their own part, called **Lens Ring**.
  - In 30- and 38-channel modes all the rings are one colour.
  - In 99-channel mode each ring has its own colour, and the aux master dimmer and strobe still apply to all of them.
  - Before, the ring colour could replace the beam colour (38 channels), or the rings were not shown at all (30 channels).
  - The halo strip round the head works in all three modes.
- **Betopper LM1915R:** the halo ring takes its own colour in 24- and 32-channel modes. The 15-channel mode has no ring channels (see the manual), so the ring is drawn unlit.
- **Zoned washes now show every LED.** A 19-LED wash whose mode controls the LEDs in three zones shows all 19 lenses, not 3.
- **Other fixtures:** a moving head with "Aura" or "Aux" colour channels (for example the Martin MAC Aura in Extended mode) gets a ring part of its own too. Its ring channels no longer change the beam colour.
- **Fixture Studio:** the Part menu has **LED rings (round each lens)** next to **LED ring (round the head)**.

Ring effects and letter macros are not drawn yet. Colour and brightness are.
