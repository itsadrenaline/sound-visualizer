## What's new

### LED acrylic panels

A new kind of fixture: a frosted acrylic panel in a frame, lit from inside by an RGBW LED strip. The glow is brightest along the edges, where the strip is, and softer in the middle. Each panel throws a little of its colour onto the floor in front of it.

- **In the Library:** search for "LED Acrylic Panel RGBW" (under Generic). It has three modes: RGBW (4 channels), Dimmer + RGBW (5) and Dimmer + RGBW + Strobe (6). It starts at 20 × 47 in (50 × 120 cm).
- **In Fixture Studio:** choose **LED acrylic panel** as the kind to make your own, with the channels your panels use. Any fixture with "acrylic panel" in its name gets this look too, including ones from SoundSwitch.
- **Size and frame per panel:** select one or more panels and use the new **Panel** section in the inspector. Set the **width** and **height** in inches, and the **frame** to white, black or silver. Each panel can be different.
- **Join panels together:** select the panels, right-click and choose **Join panels side by side** (or the button in the Panel section). They line up edge to edge in a row, starting from the leftmost one and facing the same way, like the front of a DJ booth. Panels of different widths fit together too. Then move, turn or raise the row like any other fixtures.
- The right-click menu also has **White frame**, **Black frame** and **Silver frame**.
