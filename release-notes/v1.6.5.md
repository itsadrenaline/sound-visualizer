## What's new

### Every DMX mode a fixture comes in

The Cameo Hydrabeam 400 has five modes (6, 10, 19, 32 and 56 channels), but once it was in a show you only ever saw the one it was added in. That's fixed for every fixture.

- **DMX mode in the inspector:** select a fixture and the new **DMX mode** menu lists all of its modes with their channel counts. Pick another and the fixture switches, keeping its address. If the new mode is longer and runs into the next fixture, the patch check tells you.
- **Fixture Studio opens every mode:** right-click → **Edit fixture…** now opens the fixture with all its modes, on the one your show uses. Before, it only had that one mode.
- **Fixtures from SoundSwitch** bring every mode their SoundSwitch definition has. The DMX mode menu shows them all, but SoundSwitch decides which one is used: change it in SoundSwitch's patch and the show updates. (Shows that are linked to SoundSwitch pick this up the next time they read SoundSwitch.)
- **More SoundSwitch mode names are recognised**, like "56CH", "DMX 8" or "Extended". Before, a mode with a name like that could be merged into the next mode or left out.
- **Checked across the whole Library:** all 3,050 Open Fixture Library modes and 4,692 QLC+ modes load with the right number of channels, the highest-channel modes included.

### Hydrabeams and other multi-head movers

- **Four heads in every mode:** in the 6- and 10-channel modes, where all four heads move together, a Hydrabeam 400 used to be drawn as one big head. It's now four heads moving as one. The Hydrabeam 300 has three.
- **The shared colour and dimmer reach every head:** in the 10-, 19- and 32-channel modes, the colour, dimmer and strobe are shared by all the heads. The heads didn't take them, so they stayed white or stayed lit at blackout. They now follow them. The same fix gives the ADJ Vizi Q Wash7, the Mega-Lite MW1, the Fun Generation Mr. Beam and the Clay Paky Tornado their shared colour on the beam. On the ADJ Event Bar LED and the Chauvet Intimidator Spot Duo, each head now follows the shared movement speed.
- **Library tip:** the Library has two Hydrabeam 400 entries. "Hydrabeam 400 RGBW" (Open Fixture Library) has all five modes up to 56 channels. "Hydrabeam 400 Series" (QLC+) only goes up to 32.
