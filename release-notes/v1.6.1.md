## What's new

### Disco ball

There's a new **Disco ball** in Objects ▸ Party: a mirror ball on a motor.

- **Size:** 8 to 40 in.
- **Chain or rod length:** how far below its hook it hangs, or how high its rod lifts it when it stands.
- **Turning:** still, slow, normal or fast.

Where it goes depends on where you put it. Put it under a truss bar and it hangs from the bar. Put it on a totem or stand and it sits on top. Put it on a table and it stands there. Anywhere else, it hangs from the ceiling.

**The reflections are worked out from the light that hits it.** Each mirror tile a beam lights throws a spot of that beam's colour onto the floor, ceiling or back wall, with a thin ray through the haze. As the ball turns, the spots sweep round the room. A pin spot or moving head on part of a big ball lights just that part. A wash over the whole ball lights its whole near side. Point a moving head at it and the spots take its colour.

### Hanging from the ceiling

- Right-click a fixture ▸ **Hanging from the ceiling** hangs it upside down from the ceiling, right above where it is. It stays up there when you move it. Use it for a Starburst over the dance floor, or any light on a ceiling hook.
- Choosing **Hanging (truss mode)** when there's no truss at all now hangs the light from the ceiling. Before, it said there was nothing to hang it from.
- Standing a fixture **upright** puts it back on the floor, facing up.

### More places on a truss

The crossbar of a goalpost truss now also has **Back of the crossbar, facing out**. A Starburst or any other light can go on top of the truss, under it, on the front facing out, or on the back facing out.

### Lasers

- **Colours:** lasers take the colour their colour channel picks. This fix is in 1.6.0 and is included here.
- **Patterns:** the beams change between figures like the real laser's shows: flat fans (sheets of light through the haze), cones (tunnels) and separate beams.
- **Laserworld EL-900RGB:** each of its four fans now has more lines.
