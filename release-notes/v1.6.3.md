## What's new

### Cameo Hydrabeam moves at the right speed

The Cameo Hydrabeam's heads crawled even when the show had them moving fast. Its manual says its head speed channels go **slow to fast**: 0 is slowest and 255 fastest. That's the opposite of most moving heads, and the app read them the usual way round. So "full speed" was drawn as "slowest".

- Hydrabeams from SoundSwitch and from the Library now move at the speed the show sends, in every mode. That includes the per-head speeds in the 32-channel mode, where 0-10 means "use the main speed".
- Shows you've already saved get the fix when you open them.
- A head with no speed channel of its own now follows the fixture's main speed channel.
- When SoundSwitch's ranges say which way a speed channel goes ("slow - fast"), the app now follows them, for any fixture.
- The Hydrabeam now has its real size (810 × 250 × 130 mm) and 4.5° beams. Its four heads were already drawn separately.
