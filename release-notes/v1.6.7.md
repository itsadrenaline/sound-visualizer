## What's new

### Deleted SoundSwitch fixtures stay deleted

Deleting a fixture that came from SoundSwitch could make it vanish and then come straight back. This happened when the show had a different name from its SoundSwitch project, for example a show imported from a file or saved under a new name. The app noted the deletion under the show's name, but syncs look under the SoundSwitch project's name, so the next sync added the fixture again.

- **Deletions are kept in the show itself.** They work whatever the show or its SoundSwitch project is called. The show file carries them to another Mac, and they work in the browser version too.
- **Undo** brings a deleted fixture back.
- **Bring back deleted (n):** a new button at the top of the Fixtures list brings back every SoundSwitch fixture deleted from this show. They return on the next sync.
- Copies you made of a SoundSwitch fixture, sharing its patch, aren't affected.

### If the app crashes

- **The window reloads after a crash.** If the page inside the window crashes, it reloads on its own, instead of leaving an empty window.
- **Safe mode:** if the page crashes again straight away, the app starts with an empty show. Your last show is put aside, not deleted. Open a saved show from **Shows**.
- **More gets logged:** crashes of the page, the graphics process and other helpers, plus errors in the page, all go to the log. If the app crashes for you, send the log (**Help ▸ Open Log Folder**, `main.log`) and the crash report from **Console ▸ Crash Reports** (or `~/Library/Logs/DiagnosticReports`, the newest "Sound Visualizer" file).
