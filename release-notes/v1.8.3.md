## What's new

### Record a video of the show

- **The video camera button** in the 3D view's toolbar records the show as it runs. The menu has it too: File ▸ Record Video of the Show (⇧⌘R).
- While it records, the button turns red and shows a timer. Click it again to stop and save.
- The video is an MP4 (H.264), so it plays on any Mac, phone or PC. If a computer can't make MP4, it saves a WebM instead.
- Only the 3D picture is recorded. The panels, buttons and move arrows stay out of it.
- Recording stops on its own after 10 minutes, and when you switch to the Plan.

### Pictures

- **Image** is now a **camera button**. It still saves the 3D view, or the Plan, as a PNG (⇧⌘E).

### Plan: Left and Right

- **The Plan's Side view is now two views, Left and Right**, the same as the 3D view. ⇧4 and ⇧5 pick them.
- Switching to 3D from either one looks from that side.
- Switching to the Plan from 3D while you're looking from the right side opens on Right.
- If you had the Plan on Side, it opens on Left.
