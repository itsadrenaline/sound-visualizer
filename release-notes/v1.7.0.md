## What's new

### Real sizes and looks for about 2,500 fixtures

Fixtures now take their real size, shape and housing from the Show-Designer fixture catalogue (shared with us by its author, thank you). This covers 2,466 fixtures from 11 makers: Eurolite, Chauvet DJ and Professional, ADJ, U'King, Stairville, Cameo, SHEHDS, lightmaXX, Both Lighting, Ape Labs and Blizzard. A Wash FX now looks like a Wash FX, not a generic PAR.

- **About 1,800 fixtures that weren't in the Library are now listed.** They're marked "set up from the manual": they have their real look and every DMX mode, so **Set it up in Fixture Studio** opens them ready to fill in. Simple colour channels are filled in for you; the rest are marked for you to set, with a link to the maker's page.
- **Housing colour:** fixtures that come in black, white or silver get a **Housing** menu in the inspector.
- **Chauvet DJ Freedom Par Q9** (4, 6 and 14 channels) and **STRIKE 1** (1 to 7 channels), written from their manuals.

### New kinds of fixture

Fixture Studio and the Library have four new looks:

- **Bee-eye moving heads**: big domed lenses on a face that spins.
- **Moving-head bars**: a row of small heads, each tilting on its own.
- **Scanners**: a fixed body with a moving mirror.
- **Cold spark machines**: sparks fountain up, as high as the height channel says.

### Party objects

- **Marquee letters:** big light-up letters with bulbs. LOVE, initials, a name or a number, in white, black or gold. Switch them on and off, or let them follow the room light.
- **Monogram projection:** a couple's initials or a name in a ring, projected on the dance floor or a wall, in any colour.
