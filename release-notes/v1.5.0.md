## What's new

### Edit a fixture straight from your show

Right-click a fixture (in the list or the 3D view) and **Edit fixture…** is the first item. It's also on the bar above a selected fixture (**✎ Edit**), on each row of the fixture list when you point at it, and on a group's header. It opens Fixture Studio with that fixture (your edited version, if you've made one): change it, then **Save & add to show** updates every fixture of that type in the show.

### Stands are optional

A GigBar-style bar used to always come with a 2.2 m stand. Now:

- **In Fixture Studio**, under Look, **Comes with its own stand** is off unless you tick it.
- **In your show**, right-click a fixture ▸ **Its own stand** takes it off (or puts it back), for a bar that sits on a totem, the booth or a T-bar stand from Objects.

### Fixed: lights moving or showing colour with nothing sent

- **After the demo**: stopping Play demo (by clicking a fader, for example) could leave its last frame behind. When SoundSwitch stopped sending, the rig showed those colours and positions. Stopping the demo now clears them.
- **When the show stops arriving**: moving heads swung back to the centre. They now stay where the show left them, and the lights go dark.
- **Lights on with every channel at 0**: some parts lit up by themselves (a strobe whose 0 means "open", a colour wheel whose first slot is a colour). A fixture with nothing sent is now dark.
- **Built-in programs at 0**: a fixture file whose first program starts at 0 ran a colour chase with nothing sent. 0 now means "no program".
- **Derbies** kept turning slowly while dark. They only turn when lit or when their motor channel says so.
- **Another show on the network**: Art-Net from another console or computer, on universes this show doesn't use, could be shifted onto your rig. Only the "counts universes from 1" case is lined up now.
