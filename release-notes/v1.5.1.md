## What's new

### Fewer permission prompts

The app used to look for SoundSwitch projects in your Music, Documents, Downloads and Desktop folders, and macOS asked for permission to each of the last three. Now it only looks in SoundSwitch's own folder, **Music ▸ SoundSwitch**, on its own. It no longer looks through the rest of your Music folder either, which made macOS ask for access to Apple Music. A project somewhere else is still found when SoundSwitch has it open, or when you pick it with Browse, and it's remembered from then on. At most you'll be asked once, for the folder your project is really in, plus the usual one for your local network (that's how SoundSwitch's Art-Net reaches the app).

### Edit fixture is in the right-click menu only

Right-click a fixture (in the list or the 3D view) ▸ **Edit fixture…** opens it in Fixture Studio. The ✎ Edit buttons on the list rows, group headers and the bar above a selected fixture are gone, so nothing opens by accident.
