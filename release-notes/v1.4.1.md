## What's new

### Fixed: switching to another SoundSwitch project

A show stayed stuck on the first SoundSwitch project it was made from: picking another project did nothing. Now, when SoundSwitch has a different project open, click the project name at the top:

- **Switch this show to "…"** asks what should happen to your show first, so the work you put into the stage is never lost by surprise (trusses, stands and cameras always stay, and Edit ▸ Undo puts it all back):
  - **Keep my show as it is**: the same rig with different programs. Nothing moves; the show just follows the new project.
  - **Add it to my show**: keep everything and bring in the fixtures the show doesn't have yet.
  - **Update my show to match**: fixtures on the same patch keep their place and settings; the rest are removed or added.
  - **Start a new show** from it (you're asked to save this one first).
- **Fixtures the project doesn't have get a decision, not just a warning.** If some of the show's fixtures aren't in the SoundSwitch project (none of their DMX channels are used there), SoundSwitch sends them nothing, so they'd stay dark when the show plays. The switch dialog lists them and asks what to do with them, and the SoundSwitch pill turns amber ("N not in project") with the same choices:
  - **Leave them as they are**: they stay in the show (the demo, faders or another console can still light them) and stop being flagged.
  - **Mute them**: still drawn, never lit or moved.
  - **Hide them**: not drawn; still in the show.
  - **Remove them from the show** (Edit ▸ Undo brings them back).
  Or add them in SoundSwitch on the same universe and address to light them.
- **New show from "…"** makes a separate show from the open project.
- **Open another SoundSwitch project…** makes a show from any project you pick.
- A project you pick is no longer swapped back to the one SoundSwitch has open a few seconds later.

Everything in [1.4.0](https://github.com/itsadrenaline/sound-visualizer/releases/tag/v1.4.0) (the Fixture Studio, lights on speakers and inside totems, string lights, Betopper and Rockville fixtures) is included.
