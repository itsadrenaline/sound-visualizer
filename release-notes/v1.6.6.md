## What's new

### Equinox Fusion Orbit MKII: moves, and is lit, in every mode

A user's Fusion Orbit MKII didn't move. The Library's version of it (from QLC+) put the beam's colour on a part that doesn't move. So the moving head itself stayed dark and only the LEDs on its face lit up. It also had no movement at all in the 2- and 7-channel modes. It's now rewritten from Equinox's manual (EQLED018), with all four modes:

- **2 channels:** shows 1 to 5 and sound sensitivity. The shows move the head and change its colours. Show 4, which faces forward, holds the head still.
- **7 channels:** the four pan/tilt programs move the head on their own. Beam and ring each have a dimmer and the 20 colour macros from the manual. Plus the ring's macro programs and their speed.
- **16 channels:** pan, tilt, pan/tilt speed, and the beam in RGBW with dimmer and strobe. The LED ring round the lens has its own RGB, dimmer and strobe. Plus shows and sound sensitivity.
- **37 channels:** adds fine pan and tilt and the colour macros. The ring is split into six segments, each in its own colour, all under the ring's dimmer and strobe.
- Its pan/tilt speed goes **slow to fast** (0 is slowest), as the manual says. If a show sends 0 on that channel, the head moves slowly, like the real one.
- Fusion Orbits from SoundSwitch get the same look: a beam head with the LED ring, 540° pan and 210° tilt.

To use the new version for a Fusion Orbit already in a show, select it and pick its mode again in the inspector's **DMX mode** menu, or add it again from the Library.

### Moving heads that move on their own

Moving heads with pan/tilt programs, shows or sound modes now move on their own while one is running. Before, they only moved by their pan and tilt channels. This works for every moving head in the Library that has them. Each program moves differently: circles, figure-eights, sweeps or wandering.

### Smaller fixes

- On moving heads whose light comes from LED cells rather than the head itself (ADJ XS-600, Pulse Impstar 40, Martin MAC Aura XIP and others), the beam now shows the cells' colour. Before, it stayed dark.
- An LED ring split into segments is drawn as that many arcs, each in its own colour. A ring's master dimmer and strobe now dim and strobe every segment.
