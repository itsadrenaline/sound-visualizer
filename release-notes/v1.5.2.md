## What's new

### No false "patch problems" for SoundSwitch patches

When two lights share DMX channels in your SoundSwitch project, that's how the project is set up, often on purpose: a second light on the same address copies the first. The app used to list these as "patch problems" and offer to move them, but the next sync would undo the move. They're no longer problems. A quiet line under the fixture list now says how many fixtures share channels in your SoundSwitch patch; hover over it to see which. Clashes with lights you added in the app itself are still reported as before.

A SoundSwitch mode's channel count now comes from its name when the two disagree. Before, a "6 Channel Mode" could take far more channels than 6.

### Lights drawn with all their LEDs

- **PARs and floods with many LEDs** show every LED, laid out in rings on a round PAR. A "Par RGBWAU 12x12" now has 12 LEDs, not one big lens. The count comes from the fixture's name ("12x12", "18 x 10W"), or set it yourself in Fixture Studio ▸ Look ▸ **LEDs on the face**.
- **Fixtures with several moving heads** draw one small head for each, each following its own pan and tilt. Examples: Cameo Hydrabeam, ADJ Crazy Pocket, Duo Q-Beam bars.
- **New look: two tilting bars.** For the BeamZ Double Helix and 8-beam "spider" effects. Each bar tilts on its own motor, and each LED lights in its own colour.
- **New look: LED curtain / starcloth.** For the BeamZ SparkleWall. LEDs are scattered over a dark curtain that hangs from the truss.

### Lasers change colour

A laser's colour channel now sets the colour of its beams. Before, every laser was drawn red and green whatever the colour channel said. Lasers coming from SoundSwitch are also recognised by name ("EL-900RGB", "Laserworld …"), so they're drawn as lasers and not as moving heads.

### New in the Library, from the manufacturers' manuals

- **BeamZ Double Helix 8x3W RGBW (MHL820)**: 9, 11, 12, 16 and 18 channels.
- **BeamZ SparkleWall LED36 RGBW 2x1m (151.202)** and **LED96 RGBW 3x2m (151.200)**: 8 channels.
- **Laserworld EL-900RGB**: 9 channels. Off below 50 on the mode channel, and seven colours.

A Double Helix or SparkleWall coming from SoundSwitch gets these looks too. This also applies to shows you've already saved.

### Fixes to how Library fixtures look

- Some effects were drawn as GigBars: ADJ Asteroid 1200, Hyper Gem and Revo 4, and Chauvet Hive. They're now effect boxes. Chauvet Wash FX Hex and Wash FX2 are now floods.
- Lights with "spider" in their name no longer all borrow the look of one particular spider wash.
