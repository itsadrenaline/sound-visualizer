## What's new

### New fixtures from their manuals

- **ADJ Focus Spot 4Z** (and 4Z Pearl): all three modes, Basic 16, Standard 18 and Extended 22 channels. It has the colour wheel with its scroll, the six gobos with shake and wheel spin, gobo rotation, both prisms with indexing and rotation, focus, zoom (11-22°), the dim curves and the reset/program channel. It replaces the QLC+ version.
- **Ayra ERO 706**: 11 and 15 channels. A wash moving head with seven RGBWA+UV LEDs. The 15-channel mode adds fine pan/tilt, speed, colour macros and the sound trigger.
- Searching **"ADJ"** now finds American DJ fixtures, so "ADJ Focus Spot 4" finds the Focus Spot 4Z.

### Clearing out old fixtures

- **Right-click a fixture in the Library** to open it in Fixture Studio or to take it off **Recent**. Right-click the Recent heading to clear the whole list.
- **Delete fixtures from My Fixtures:** right-click one and choose **Delete from My Fixtures…**, or use **Delete…** in its details. Fixtures already in your shows keep working.
- **Importing a corrected fixture file replaces the old one.** If My Fixtures already has a fixture with the same maker and name, saving the imported version updates it, and the copies in your show pick up the change. Before, you got a second copy next to the old one.

### Fixture files written by an AI

You can import Open Fixture Library JSON files written by hand or by an AI (Library ▸ **Import…**). These files often list a channel without describing it. Those channels now get their job from their name ("Gobo Wheel", "Prism 1 Rotation", "Pan/Tilt Speed") and are marked as guessed. Any it can't work out are marked for you to set from the manual. The import report says which ones. The maker is read from the file too, where before it showed "Unknown".

### SoundSwitch

Channels named the way manuals print them, such as "Dimmer Red 0-100%", "Master dimmer 0-100%" or "Shutter/strobe channel", are now recognised. Before, a fixture like that could show a plain white beam that never changed colour.
