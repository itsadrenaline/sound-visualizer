## What's new

### The Plan: place things exactly

Press **Plan** at the top left of the 3D view (or **P**) for a flat, schematic drawing of your show, like a lighting plot. Press **3D** (or **P** again) to go back.

- **Top, Front and Side.** The top view is where you lay things out. Front and side show heights: drag a loose fixture up or down there.
- **Drag to move.** What's on an object comes with it. Drop a fixture on a totem top or a truss bar and it attaches there (a blue ring shows where). Drag on empty floor to select a group.
- **Exact positions.** While you drag, the position shows next to it. Select two things to see the distance between them. Arrow keys move the selection: 10 cm (3″), or 1 m (1 ft) with Shift.
- **Turn objects** with the round handle (15° steps, hold Shift for any angle), or press **R** for 90°.
- **Line things up.** Without snapping, a dragged item lines up with its neighbours' centres (blue guides).
- **Labels** show fixture names or their patch, or turn them off. **Image** saves the Plan as a picture to send to the venue.
- Drag fixtures from the Library and objects from the Objects tab straight onto the Plan.
- Right-click, double-click and the floating bar work as they do in 3D.

### Grid and snap

The **Grid** menu sets the grid size (10 cm to 1 m, or 6″ to 4′) and **Snap to the grid** (**G**). With snapping on, whatever you drag lands on the grid, in the Plan and in 3D, where the grid shows on the floor. Arrow keys then move one square.

### Metric or imperial, everywhere

Choose **m** or **ft** at the top right (or View ▸ Units). Every length follows: positions, heights, object sizes, the ceiling, fixture and panel sizes, the Library, Fixture Studio and the CSV patch list. Objects with automatic names follow too ("Totem 1.83 m" ↔ "Totem 6 ft"). Metric is the default.

You can type either system in any length field, whichever is chosen: 6', 6 ft 2, 74in, 1.8 m or 180cm.

### Lock things in place

**Lock** fixtures or objects so they can't be moved by accident. Use the padlock on the floating bar, right-click ▸ **Lock in place**, or **⇧⌘L**.

- A locked item can still be selected, but dragging, the arrow keys, the move fields, turning, resizing, lining up and distributing all leave it where it is.
- A padlock shows next to it in the lists and on the Plan. Click it to unlock.
