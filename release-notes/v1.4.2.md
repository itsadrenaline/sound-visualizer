## What's new

### Fixed: making a show from SoundSwitch did nothing for some rigs

If your SoundSwitch project had a laser, a derby, a strobe or blinder, or a pixel matrix panel, **Create show** in the New Show wizard didn't work: the button seemed to do nothing, and closing the wizard left an empty show with the project's name. Switching a show to another project failed the same way. Every kind of fixture now gets a place (effects hang from the truss in front of the movers, fog machines at the back), and if making a show ever fails, the wizard says why instead of doing nothing.

### Fixed: the wrong SoundSwitch project being picked as "open"

SoundSwitch can keep files open in a project you had open earlier, and the app sometimes picked that one (the pill said SoundSwitch had a different project open than the one on screen). It now picks the project SoundSwitch is really using, and if it still gets it wrong, the SoundSwitch pill lists the other projects SoundSwitch has open: click **Use "…"** to follow the right one.

### About Sound Visualizer

Sound Visualizer ▸ About, Help ▸ About, or **? Learn ▸ About** shows your version, build and system, with **Copy details** to paste into a message when you report a problem.
