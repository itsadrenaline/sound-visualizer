## What's fixed

### Getting around in 3D

- **Orbiting up and down no longer zooms in.** Holding Space and dragging up or down (or using the Turn buttons) keeps the same distance from what you're looking at. Before, the camera was pushed back under the ceiling and above the floor every frame, which pulled it closer each time.
- **You can look straight down on the whole show.** The camera can go up past the ceiling (it's see-through from above) and zoom out as far as you like from there. The Top view works the same way. The camera still never goes through the floor: orbiting down stops at floor level, at the same distance.

### Switching between 3D and the Plan

- **The Plan and 3D now pick up where the other left off.**
  - From the Plan's Front view, 3D opens looking from the front at the same spot and zoom. Top and Side work the same way.
  - From 3D, the Plan opens on the side that matches how you're looking: Top when you're looking down, Front or Side when you're looking straight at them. From an angle in between, it stays on the side it was last on.
  - The Plan opens centred and zoomed on what the 3D view was showing.

### Plan view buttons

- **The on-screen camera buttons are back in the Plan.** Zoom in and out, move the view up, down, left and right, and ⌂ shows everything. You don't need a scroll wheel or trackpad.
- **New Plan keys:** + and − zoom, and Home shows everything. With nothing selected, the arrow keys move the view. With something selected, they move the selection, as before.
