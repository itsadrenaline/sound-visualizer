## What's new

### Party objects and screens

The Objects list has two new sections, **Party** and **Screens**.

- **Dance floor** (now under Party). Tick **LED tiles that light up** for a light-up floor: the tiles run colour chases, flipping checkers and ripples. The ready-made "LED dance floor 15 × 15 ft" already has them on.
- **Balloon arch**, in six colour sets: gold and white, pink and white, blue and silver, black and gold, red and white, or rainbow.
- **360 photo booth**: a round platform with a camera arm and ring light that turns slowly.
- **Neon sign**: your own words in glowing neon, in eight colours, on a stand or at any height.
- **TV**: a flat screen from 43 to 98 in, on a floor stand or on its feet on a table.
- **Projection screens, LED walls and TVs can now show something.** Choose what in the object's settings under **Showing**:
  - **Colours**: slow washes of colour.
  - **Visuals**: a club-style tunnel pulsing at 124 bpm.
  - **Picture**: any photo or graphic. It's kept in the show file.
  - **Video**: an MP4, MOV or WebM, playing on a loop. Videos are big, so a copy stays on this computer. If you open the show on another computer, choose the video again.
  - **Words**: "Happy Birthday", a name, the venue, in eight colours.

### Fixtures facing out on the front of a truss

Lights can now clamp to the front of a truss on their backs, so they face the audience instead of pointing up. A Double Helix, for example, then tilts its beams up and down across the room. Choose **Front of the crossbar, facing out** (or front of a leg, front of a totem, front or back of a truss box) under **Attached to**. Or right-click a light that's already on a clamp or facade and tick **Face out (on its back)**.

### Lasers: the right colours, and four fans for the EL-900RGB

- A laser now always takes the colour its colour channel picks. This includes SoundSwitch lasers whose colour wheel is in a different part from the laser itself, and colours given only by name, in English or Dutch ("wit", "groen").
- Colour settings with several colours, like "Red + Green", show beams in each colour. "Multicolour" settings cycle through all of them.
- The **Laserworld EL-900RGB** is drawn with its four apertures across a curved front. Each throws its own fan of beams, sweeping on its own, like the real one. Fixture Studio has a new **Apertures** setting for other multi-aperture lasers.

### ADJ Starburst

- The **ADJ Starburst** is in the Library, written from ADJ's manual: 12 channels, and six colours (red, green, blue, white, yellow and purple).
- It has its own look: a ball covered in lenses on a base. Thin beams shoot out in every direction, and the ball turns with the rotation channel, either way. Starbursts coming from SoundSwitch get this look too.
- The Library listed it as "Starbust" before, so searching for "Starburst" didn't find it. That's fixed.

### Smaller fixes

- Derbies and other effects from the Library now turn at the speed and in the direction their rotation channel sets.
